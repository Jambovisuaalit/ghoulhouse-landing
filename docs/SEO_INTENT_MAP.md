## Cannibalization guardrails

- `/some-sisallontuotanto` owns the generic service query; `/some-12` owns the named package; `/some-sisallontuotanto/hinta` owns price intent.
- Vertical Social pages own commercial vertical intent. The construction guide remains informational and must link to, not compete with, `/rakennusyrityksille`.
- `/verkkosivut-yritykselle` owns generic website-service intent. Vertical website pages own industry modifiers.
- `/referenssit` and `/tyot/...` exist to prove work, not to duplicate service-page copy.
- New Helsinki/Espoo/Vantaa or other location pages require: verified service area, local proof, unique user value, and Search Console demand.
