# SEO QA — 2026-10-06

## Decision

Do not expand the URL set. Improve proof and conversion quality on the existing 19 indexable URLs first.

## Verified technical state in repository

- Canonical production host logic exists.
- Non-canonical managed host redirects to the canonical host.
- Preview/non-canonical requests receive an X-Robots-Tag noindex directive.
- robots.txt and sitemap use the same production indexing predicate.
- Page-level robots metadata is now routed through `indexableRobots()`, so `SITE_INDEXABLE=false` cannot be contradicted by page metadata.
- Sitemap contains the homepage plus 18 purpose-specific routes.
- Commercial pages have canonical metadata and unique H1/title structures.

## Content QA

### Pass

- Social service, package and price intents are separated.
- Construction, LVI and renovation Social pages have industry-specific copy rather than city-keyword variants.
- Website service, price and vertical intents are separated.
- Guides are informational and can support commercial pages through internal links.
- Claims generally avoid invented client results.

### Watch

- The strongest gap is external proof, not word count. Current copy explains the method well but cannot substitute for published customer work.
- `/referenssit` should become a stronger proof hub as soon as publishable customer cases exist.
- Industry pages should receive real project examples, service-area facts and approved images when available.
- Do not add generic AI-expanded paragraphs solely to increase page length.

### Hold

Do not create location landing pages or additional near-duplicate service pages until at least one of these exists:

1. Search Console query/impression evidence,
2. a verified local customer/reference,
3. a materially different service or buying situation.

## Conversion QA target

Commercial page funnel:

`page_view → primary CTA click → form_start → form_submit → form_success`

Required dimensions:

- `page_path`
- `service`
- `intent`

Primary metrics:

- CTA click-through rate
- form-start rate
- form completion rate
- qualified lead rate by service
